Solution Overview

Arabsat — the Arab Satellite Communications Organization — is one of the leading satellite operators in the MENA region, serving over 100 countries with a fleet of geostationary communication satellites. Founded in 1976 and headquartered in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, Arabsat operates high-power satellites at strategic orbital positions, delivering broadcasting, broadband, and government-grade connectivity services. ADMC distributes Arabsat connectivity solutions to system integrators and enterprise clients across the UAE and MENA.

Major Benefits

  • Extensive satellite fleet providing high-power transponder capacity across MENA, Europe, and Africa.
  • Proven infrastructure for broadcast distribution, managed services, and government connectivity.
  • Geostationary coverage enabling reliable connectivity for remote, maritime, and enterprise sites.
  • Specification and procurement support backed by ADMC's regional distribution capability.

Core Capability Areas

Broadcasting & DTH Services

Arabsat's BADR satellite position at 26°E hosts one of the largest direct-to-home broadcasting neighbourhoods in the Arab world, distributing hundreds of channels to hundreds of millions of viewers across MENA and Europe.

Broadband & Enterprise Connectivity

High-throughput satellite (HTS) capacity supports broadband internet access and enterprise private network services for sites beyond terrestrial fibre reach, including remote industrial, hospitality, and government locations.

Government & Defence Communications

Dedicated bandwidth and secure connectivity solutions for government agencies, defence programmes, and critical national infrastructure requiring resilient, sovereign satellite communications.

Maritime & Aeronautical Connectivity

Satellite services enabling voice, data, and crew welfare connectivity for maritime vessels and commercial aviation operating across the MENA and Indian Ocean regions.
