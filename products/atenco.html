Solution Overview

Atenco Power Tech is a Taiwanese manufacturer specialising in uninterruptible power supplies (UPS), automatic voltage regulators (AVR), VRLA batteries, and outdoor power enclosures. Their portfolio covers the full spectrum of power protection — from line-interactive UPS for office and education environments to true online double-conversion systems for mission-critical datacenters and industrial installations. ADMC distributes Atenco power solutions across the UAE and MENA, providing specification support and supply chain coordination for integrators and end-users in demanding uptime environments.

Major Benefits

  • True online double-conversion UPS isolates sensitive loads from sags, surges, and frequency drift.
  • DSP-controlled architecture with AVR, SNMP/USB monitoring, and generator compatibility to reduce downtime.
  • AGM VRLA batteries with 5–12 year design life and TTP intercell connections for reliable backup energy.
  • Outdoor UPS (PowTerra) with integrated thermal management and IP-rated enclosure for harsh environments.
  • ADMC regional support covering specification, procurement, and supply coordination across the UAE and MENA.

Core Capability Areas

Online UPS Systems (Single & Three Phase)

True double-conversion online UPS delivers pure sine-wave output at constant voltage and frequency, fully isolating connected equipment from grid instability, brownouts, spikes, and outages. Available in single-phase and three-phase configurations for servers, telecoms, medical, and industrial loads.

Line-Interactive UPS

High-efficiency line-interactive models with built-in AVR correct voltage sags and swells without switching to battery, extending runtime and battery life for IT equipment, network closets, and office environments.

VRLA Batteries (ATN GP Series)

AGM (Absorbed Glass Mat) sealed lead-acid batteries with optimised plate design and high-purity electrolyte. Float design life 5–10 years (Standard) or 10–12 years (Long Life) at 25°C. TTP intercell connections and heat-sealed case deliver reliable high-rate discharge performance in UPS backup and standby power applications.

Outdoor UPS — PowTerra

Double-conversion online UPS housed in an air-conditioned, IP-rated outdoor cabinet with four-layer thermal management, dust and waterproof protection, and PFC input circuitry. Input range 115–295 VAC. Designed for telecoms base stations, outdoor infrastructure, and sites without climate-controlled equipment rooms.

AVR Voltage Stabilisers

Precision automatic voltage regulators providing real-time correction of sags, surges, and brownouts for sensitive loads in schools, laboratories, broadcast sites, and industrial facilities. Available single- and three-phase with CE-aligned safety, over/under-voltage, overload, and short-circuit protection with auto recovery.
