IDCA DCOS Framework

Datacenter Facility Certification

IDCA's Data Center Operating Standard (DCOS) provides the globally recognised tiering framework used to classify, certify, and benchmark datacenter facilities. Each tier defines specific requirements for power redundancy, cooling paths, and operational availability — giving owners, investors, and tenants a clear, independent measure of infrastructure resilience.

Tier 1

Basic Site Infrastructure

Availability 99.671%
Max Downtime / Year ~28.8 hrs

Single path for power and cooling with no redundant components. Suitable for small, non-critical environments where planned outages are acceptable.

Tier 2

Redundant Capacity Components

Availability 99.741%
Max Downtime / Year ~22 hrs

Redundant components (UPS modules, generators, cooling units) along a single distribution path, reducing unplanned downtime risk while still requiring maintenance shutdowns.

Tier 3

Concurrently Maintainable

Availability 99.982%
Max Downtime / Year ~1.6 hrs

Multiple power and cooling distribution paths with one active at a time, allowing planned maintenance of any component without shutting down IT load. The most prevalent tier for enterprise and colocation facilities.

Tier 4

Fault Tolerant

Availability 99.995%
Max Downtime / Year ~26 min

Fully redundant, simultaneously active infrastructure. Any single failure — planned or unplanned — cannot interrupt IT operations. Designed for mission-critical and hyperscale environments.

What the Certification Process Covers

IDCA facility audits assess the complete physical infrastructure stack: power (utility feeds, generators, UPS, PDUs), cooling (CRAC/CRAH, chillers, free-cooling), structured cabling and network distribution, physical security, and operational processes. The outcome is a formal DCOS certification report suitable for regulatory submissions, investor due diligence, and ongoing benchmarking.

Professional Development

Personal Certification Programs

IDCA's individual certification pathway is the industry standard for validating datacenter competency at every career level. From practitioners entering the field to senior consultants and executives, the program builds a common language across design, operations, IT, and energy disciplines.

CDCP

Foundation Level

Certified Data Center Professional

The entry point to IDCA's certification framework. Covers the fundamentals of datacenter design, power and cooling infrastructure, structured cabling, energy efficiency, and operational best practice. Ideal for engineers, IT professionals, and facilities personnel moving into datacenter roles.

  • DC design principles & physical infrastructure
  • Power: utility, UPS, generators, PDUs
  • Cooling: CRAC/CRAH, containment strategies
  • Cabling standards and pathways
  • Efficiency metrics (PUE, WUE, CUE)
  • Introduction to DCOS tier framework
CDCS

Specialist Level

Certified Data Center Specialist

An intermediate credential for practitioners with hands-on datacenter experience. Candidates pursue in-depth knowledge in one or more specialist domains — design, operations, IT, or energy — demonstrating competency in managing and optimising real-world datacenter environments.

  • Advanced power & cooling design
  • Capacity planning and management
  • DCIM tools and monitoring
  • Incident response and change management
  • Vendor-neutral operations frameworks
  • Sustainability and energy management
CDCE

Expert Level

Certified Data Center Expert

The pinnacle of IDCA's individual certification program. Designed for senior practitioners, independent consultants, and executives responsible for strategic datacenter decisions. Demonstrates mastery across all DCOS domains and the ability to lead design, transformation, and compliance programs at scale.

  • Enterprise datacenter strategy & governance
  • Full DCOS compliance and audit readiness
  • Multi-site and hyperscale planning
  • Business continuity and risk frameworks
  • Financial modelling and TCO analysis
  • Leading certification programs for organisations
