IDCA Standards Support
IDCA AE360® Standard

Datacenter Facility Certification

IDCA's AE360® standard, built on the Infinity Paradigm® framework, provides the globally recognised Grade Level system used to classify, certify, and benchmark data centers and digital infrastructure through SDS Certification. Grades run from G4 (baseline compliance) up to the single-site ceiling of G1, with G0 reserved for ecosystem-level achievement beyond any one facility — giving owners, investors, and tenants a clear, independent measure of infrastructure confidence.

Grade 4 (G4®)

Baseline Compliance

Risk Exposure Highest Acceptable
Achievable At Single-Site Level

The minimum acceptable standard, reflecting the highest allowable level of risk, insecurity, inefficiency, and vulnerability across the infrastructure stack.

Grade 3 (G3®)

Average Achievement

Risk Exposure Average
Achievable At Single-Site Level

Reflects average exposure to risk, insecurity, inefficiency, and chances of failure relative to the wider industry.

Grade 2 (G2®)

Excellent Performance

Risk Exposure Low
Achievable At Single-Site Level

An excellent-level grade demonstrating low exposure to risk, insecurity, inefficiency, and vulnerability. A strong target for enterprise and colocation facilities.

Grade 1 (G1®)

Highest Single-Site Grade

Risk Exposure Lowest
Achievable At Single-Site Level

The highest grade attainable by a single data center node — the lowest levels of risk, insecurity, inefficiency, and vulnerability achievable at one physical location.

What the Certification Process Covers

IDCA's SDS Certification assesses risk and performance across the Application Ecosystem®'s seven interconnected layers — Application, Platform, Compute, IT Infrastructure, Site Facility Infrastructure, Site, and Topology — spanning power, cooling, structured cabling, physical security, and operational process. The outcome is a formal Grade Level and Efficacy Score Rating (ESR®) suitable for regulatory submissions, investor due diligence, and ongoing benchmarking.

Professional Development

Personal Certification Programmes

IDCA's individual certification pathway is the industry standard for validating datacenter competency at every career level. From practitioners entering the field to senior consultants and executives, the programme builds a common language across design, operations, IT, and energy disciplines.

DCIS

Foundation Level

Data Center Infrastructure Specialist

The entry point to IDCA's certification framework. Covers the fundamentals of data center design, power and cooling infrastructure, structured cabling, energy efficiency, and operational best practice. Ideal for engineers, IT professionals, and facilities personnel moving into data center roles.

  • DC design principles & physical infrastructure
  • Power: utility, UPS, generators, PDUs
  • Cooling: CRAC/CRAH, containment strategies
  • Cabling standards and pathways
  • Efficiency metrics (PUE, WUE, CUE)
  • Introduction to IDCA's Grade Level framework
DCOS

Specialist Level

Data Center Operations Specialist

An intermediate credential for practitioners with hands-on data center experience, focused on day-to-day operations, capacity management, and incident response across live facility environments.

  • Advanced power & cooling design
  • Capacity planning and management
  • DCIM tools and monitoring
  • Incident response and change management
  • Vendor-neutral operations frameworks
  • Sustainability and energy management
DCA

Authority Level

Data Center Authority

The pinnacle of IDCA's individual certification pathway. Designed for senior practitioners, independent consultants, and executives responsible for strategic data center decisions. Demonstrates mastery across the Application Ecosystem® and the ability to lead design, transformation, and compliance programmes at scale.

  • Enterprise datacenter strategy & governance
  • Full Application Ecosystem compliance and audit readiness
  • Multi-site and hyperscale planning
  • Business continuity and risk frameworks
  • Financial modelling and TCO analysis
  • Leading certification programmes for organisations
AIFS

Specialist Level · AI Infrastructure

AI Factory Specialist

IDCA's newest credential, purpose-built for the AI infrastructure era. Validates the skills to design, build, and operate high-density AI factory environments — from GPU cluster power and liquid cooling to the operational demands of AI-scale compute.

  • AI factory power & liquid cooling design
  • GPU cluster infrastructure planning
  • High-density compute operations
  • AI workload capacity management
  • Sustainability for AI-scale facilities
  • Application Ecosystem alignment for AI infrastructure
