Solution Overview

ADMC partnered with Sabrewing Aircraft Company of Oxnard, California, to bring forward next-generation unmanned heavy-lift eVTOL commercial cargo aircraft. The programme is aimed at transforming how air cargo is moved while helping customers prepare for future logistics capability needs.

Major Benefits

  • Vertical takeoff and landing support for constrained or unimproved sites.
  • Heavy-lift payload profiles for both VTOL and conventional operating modes.
  • Lower expected operating and maintenance cost than legacy regional cargo aircraft.
  • 24-hour operations, universal-fuel turbine concepts, and folding wings for tight spaces.

Core Capability Areas

Payload Flexibility

Sabrewing materials highlight VTOL payload of 5,400 pounds and conventional payload above 10,000 pounds.

Operational Reach

The concept targets long-range cargo missions, including operation into difficult weather and remote locations.

Loading Practicality

Nose-ramp loading and low squat geometry reduce dependence on forklifts or pallet jacks.

Commercial Aviation Vision

The programme positions unmanned cargo aircraft as a new route to profit, access, and logistics continuity.
