Solution Overview

Starline, a Legrand brand, focuses on adaptable overhead power systems. ADMC uses this portfolio for scalable and serviceable critical power architectures in data-intensive environments.

Major Benefits

  • Amp range coverage from 40A to 1250A for varied deployment demands.
  • Maintenance-focused track busbar design supporting serviceability.
  • Compliance pathways aligned with UL, ETL listed, and CE-marked programs.
  • Fast reconfiguration support for changing floor-level power demand.
