Power & Busbar Solutions

Flexible and high-reliability power distribution frameworks for mission-critical and high-density datacenter environments.

Power Infrastructure Portfolio

ADMC distributes industrial-grade power infrastructure products to system integrators across the UAE and MENA, covering the full power chain from overhead busbar distribution through UPS protection and rack-level PDU management. The portfolio is engineered for mission-critical datacenters, carrier facilities, and high-density enterprise environments.

Overhead Busbar Distribution

Track busway and plug-in busway systems delivering tap-off flexibility from 40A to 1250A, enabling fast capacity changes without floor disruption or civil works.

Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS)

Single and three-phase UPS systems providing power conditioning, battery backup, and load continuity for critical infrastructure during grid interruptions or anomalies.

Power Distribution Units (PDUs)

Intelligent and metered rack PDUs delivering granular circuit protection, outlet-level switching, and real-time consumption monitoring for per-rack power governance.

Energy Management & Monitoring

Revenue-grade metering and BMS/DCIM-integrated management platforms for facility-wide power visibility, capacity forecasting, and efficiency reporting.

What's Included

  • Overhead busbar and busway product range for flexible, high-capacity power distribution
  • UPS systems spanning edge-site single-phase units through large three-phase datacenter installations
  • Intelligent and metered PDUs for rack-level circuit protection and consumption visibility
  • DC power distribution alternatives for modern telecom and datacenter power architectures
  • Energy monitoring solutions with BMS and DCIM integration readiness
  • Vendor specification and procurement support through ADMC's regional distribution network
