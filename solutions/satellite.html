Satellite Solutions

Reliable satellite-enabled intelligence designed to improve monitoring continuity, decision speed, and operational confidence.

Satellite Partnership

ADMC collaborates with advanced Earth-observation partners to deliver dependable satellite intelligence programs. The model is centered on Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) capabilities that maintain visibility in all weather, through cloud cover, and during day or night operations.

Reliability

All-weather SAR monitoring enables 24/7 location visibility for infrastructure, security, and critical operations.

Timeliness

High revisit frequency supports faster detection of change and more responsive field and planning decisions.

Access

Flexible data access and delivery pathways provide the right information at the right operational moment.

What ADMC Delivers

  • Use-case mapping for surveillance, monitoring, and risk-intelligence workflows
  • SAR data integration planning aligned to enterprise and mission systems
  • Commercial and partner coordination for scalable rollout models
  • Program support from pilot phase through operational adoption
  • Continuity-focused enablement for day/night and adverse-weather conditions

ICEYE Capability Context

The partnership model includes ICEYE SAR technology development direction: compact satellite platforms, imaging-mode versatility, and iterative improvements in payload software, subsystem performance, communication links, and power optimization. This supports both high-resolution and wide-swath imaging use cases for broader mission coverage.
