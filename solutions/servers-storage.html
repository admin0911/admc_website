Servers & Storage Solutions

Open, high-density compute and storage architectures designed for virtualisation, AI growth, and operational efficiency.

What We Supply

ADMC sources and distributes open, standards-based server and storage hardware to system integrators across the UAE and MENA. The portfolio spans mainstream enterprise compute through GPU-accelerated AI infrastructure, giving integrators access to high-density, low-lock-in architectures that scale from virtualisation to AI workloads.

High-Density Storage Options

Support for 45-bay, 60-bay, and 90-bay top-loading designs plus dense 2U configurations.

Cloud-Defined Storage Readiness

Server-based storage operations with cloud control planes that reduce complexity and overhead.

Operational Cost Focus

Align architecture to lower total storage spend and simplify lifecycle management at scale.

  • Platform sizing for virtualisation, enterprise compute, and storage growth patterns
  • CPU/GPU and NVMe-aligned architecture for performance-sensitive workloads
  • Server-centric storage models that reduce dependence on traditional array complexity
  • Vendor specification and commercial procurement support through ADMC's regional distribution network

Solution Benefits
