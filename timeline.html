42 Years of Growth.

From a trading and contracting enterprise founded in Riyadh in 1984, ADMC has grown into a regional infrastructure and technology distribution partner operating across the Gulf and MENA. Each milestone reflects our commitment to practical delivery, long-term partnerships, and continuous investment in the region's technology capabilities.

ADMC Growth Milestones

A four-decade journey from a trading foundation to a regional infrastructure and technology distribution partner serving enterprise, telecoms, and government programs across MENA.

2018

UAE Expansion

ADMC IT Solutions LLC

Launched in the UAE to strengthen data center and ICT infrastructure solutions, expanding the group's technology capabilities and regional presence.

2020–25

Regional Growth

Middle East Expansion

Expanded regional footprint with new offices in Qatar, Egypt, Lebanon, Jordan, Oman, Kuwait, Bahrain, Iraq, Kenya, and Jeddah to strengthen market presence and enhance delivery across key Middle East markets.

1984

Foundation

Arabian Development & Marketing Co.

Established in Saudi Arabia as the group's original foundation, focused on trading and technology distribution, marking the beginning of ADMC's regional journey.

2020

Operational Diversification

ADMC Logistics

Formed in the UAE to improve warehousing, fulfillment, and supply-chain management supporting the group's growing infrastructure projects.

2026

ADMC adopted a refreshed brand identity while preserving its execution-first delivery ethos, strengthening its position as a regional IT and infrastructure solutions provider.
